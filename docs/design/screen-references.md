# Mimo references in the Penwise iPad workbook

The numbers follow the order in each 20-screen contact sheet. Closely related screenshots describe different states of the same Penwise component.

| Workbook and Premium references | Where to look in Penwise |
|---|---|
| 1 feature overview | My workbook → Explore Penwise Premium shows the daily-plan and ad-free benefits. |
| 2 subscription offer | The paywall shows the local price, annual trial eligibility and renewal terms for each product. |
| 3 unlocked acknowledgement | A verified purchase opens the Premium workbook confirmation. |
| 4 trial acknowledgement | An active introductory annual transaction adds its actual end date and subscription management. |
| 5 lesson path | Each course map shows upcoming, current and completed letters. |
| 6 profile entry | My workbook opens the local learner dashboard. |
| 7–8 first field, empty and filled | Birth year starts blank. Choosing a year enables the next step when the age category is known. |
| 9–10 second field, unanswered and answered | In the year of the 18th birthday a second picker confirms whether the birthday has passed. |
| 11–12 name, empty and filled | The optional workbook name appears on its cover in My workbook. |
| 13–14 new and returning profiles | Practice journal changes from a first-letter invitation to actual scores, attempts and streaks. |
| 15 certificates | Course milestones count completed letters and show a rosette for a finished workbook. |
| 16 edit profile | My workbook lets the learner rename their workbook and change writing preferences. |
| 17 preferences and purchases | The daily goal, practice reminder, restore and subscription controls share one settings screen. |
| 18 support and account actions | Privacy, contact, terms and confirmed local deletion are grouped together. |
| 19–20 light and dark appearance | The appearance picker previews Light, Dark and System throughout the workbook. |

| Onboarding and practice references | Where to look in Penwise |
|---|---|
| 1 splash, 2 getting started, 3 welcome | The fox-and-pen mark, workbook introduction and progress indicator open onboarding. |
| 4 learning goal | Choose everyday notes, school or study, or cursive writing. |
| 5–6 experience levels | The selected experience changes the explanation of tracing, practice or memory tests. |
| 7 daily commitment | Choose the number of minutes per day alongside a handwriting style. |
| 8–9 new and continuing course | The map highlights the next letter and completed tiles update the chapter progress. |
| 10 learner profile | My workbook gathers saved preferences, practice statistics and milestones. |
| 11–12 activity preview and expanded detail | Tap a letter to see its shape and the practice and test previews. |
| 13 lesson introduction | Guided lessons demonstrate the stroke before the learner writes. |
| 14 streak celebration | The Home streak sheet marks actual practice days over the past week. |
| 15 completion | A completed letter shows the fox, stars and measured results. Word practice has its own tap-to-finish summary. |
| 16 partial course progress | The course cover and chapter progress bar show letters completed so far. |
| 17 confirmation | Destructive workbook actions ask for confirmation and describe which data will be cleared. |
| 18 storefront | Premium presents the available StoreKit products with a price and a single purchase action per option. |
| 19–20 new and returning practice dashboards | Home opens the handwriting check and plan. After a scored letter, Daily review offers that letter again. Reports show recent checks once two have been completed. |
