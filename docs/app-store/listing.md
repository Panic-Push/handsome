## Description

Penwise is a handwriting coach for iPad and Apple Pencil. Write on real
ruled lines and get instant, specific feedback: not just a score, but the one
thing to fix next, like "Bring the bottom of the o down to the baseline."

TAKE THE 3-MINUTE CHECK
Write a few letters, a word and a sentence. Penwise measures letter shape,
stroke direction, size, baseline, spacing, smoothness and speed, then builds
a daily plan around your weakest areas.

THREE STYLES
• Normal: clear everyday print
• Classic: upright joined-up cursive
• Traditional: slanted cursive with loops
Every letter a–z, capitals A–Z and digits 0–9.

LEARN, PRACTICE, TEST
• Watch each letter being written, then trace it with arrows and start dots
• Practise over a faint model, then on plain lines
• Write from memory and see how far you've come

WORDS AND SENTENCES
Move from letters to words and full sentences, scored letter by letter for
size, baseline and spacing.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your handwriting stays on your iPad.

PENWISE PREMIUM
Open every activity in your daily plan and practise without ads. Choose yearly (with a free week for eligible subscribers), monthly, or a one-time lifetime unlock.
